IMEI TRAINING – NGUYEN KIM

🎯 IMEI Training Objective

Accurate IMEI management ensures correct stock, reduces transaction errors, and improves customer service. Follow our 3 characters through this IMEI learning journey!

👥 Main Characters

Nam – Trainer

Years of experience in SAP operations and warehouse management.

Linh – Rookie

A new warehouse staff member, energetic and eager to learn.

IMEI Bot

System assistant that appears whenever an error occurs.

📘 12 Steps – Key Learning Moments

Welcome to IMEI Training

IMEI is the unique identification of each product. Correct IMEI management ensures correct stock and accurate transactions. Today, we will learn the full IMEI workflow in SAP & PDW.

Nam: “Linh, are you ready? Today we learn IMEI from A–Z.”
Linh: “Yes, I want to understand it clearly to avoid mistakes.”

Important Step: Select the Serial Number Tab

When receiving or issuing IMEI stock, always go to the “Serial Number” tab and enter each IMEI one by one.

Nam: “This is where all IMEIs must be entered. Never skip this tab.”
Linh: “Got it. If I miss this tab, everything is wrong.”

Scan IMEI Line by Line

SAP does not auto-jump lines. Each IMEI must be scanned into the correct line manually.

Nam: “Scan one IMEI per line, don’t scan continuously.”
Linh: “Yes, I’m scanning carefully line by line.”

Error: IMEI Quantity Too Low

If the number of scanned IMEIs is lower than the required GR quantity, SAP stops the process immediately.

IMEI Bot: “⚠️ Error: IMEI quantity < GR quantity.”
Nam: “Check if any IMEI is missing.”
Linh: “I’ll add the missing IMEI now.”

Check IMEI Before Warehouse Transfer

Always check which warehouse an IMEI belongs to using ZMB52 before performing any transfer.

Nam: “Always confirm the IMEI’s warehouse first.”
Linh: “I’m checking the IMEI list now.”

Error: IMEI Not Found in Source Warehouse

If the scanned IMEI does not exist in the source warehouse, SAP blocks the transfer.

IMEI Bot: “❌ Error 03 – IMEI not in selected warehouse.”
Linh: “Why is it in another warehouse?”
Nam: “Check ZMB52 again, it belongs elsewhere.”

Understand Why IMEI Errors Happen

This error occurs when the IMEI is in another warehouse, already issued, or locked in another status.

IMEI Bot: “IMEI not found in this warehouse. Please confirm its original warehouse.”
Linh: “Understood. I must always check IMEI beforehand.”

Issue Stock with Correct IMEI

IMEIs must match the OD exactly. Wrong IMEI = immediate stock discrepancy.

Nam: “Enter IMEI exactly as on the OD.”
Linh: “I’m matching the IMEI now.”

IMEI Accepted

When IMEI is correctly entered, SAP displays a green message: IMEI accepted.

Linh: “I see the green message. That means it’s good, right?”
Nam: “Correct. That means IMEI is valid.”

Warning: IMEI Already Used

If the IMEI has been used before, SAP will block it.

IMEI Bot: “❌ Error 02 – IMEI already used.”
Linh: “Oh, this IMEI was returned by a customer before?”
Nam: “Correct. Choose an IMEI that is still available.”

Check IMEI Inventory Using ZMB52

Use ZMB52 to verify IMEI location, status, and availability.

Linh: “ZMB52 shows this IMEI is still in warehouse 001.”
Nam: “Good. This report must be checked daily.”

Training Completed

Mastering IMEI helps reduce errors, speed up reconciliation, and improve customer experience.

Nam: “Congratulations Linh, you’ve completed the IMEI workflow!”
Linh: “Thank you, I feel much more confident now.”
IMEI Bot: “Status: Training Completed. Expected error rate: -90%.”
